The short answer

Five steps: 1) check you are allowed to record; 2) record from close to the speaker and test first; 3) transcribe, on your own device with Vox.md or with another tool; 4) review the text against the audio; 5) save it as a study note. An automatic transcript is a draft: it needs your corrections before you study from it.

The method works with any recorder and any transcription tool. The Vox.md steps follow the models and Mac documentation and the app’s shipped behavior. Outside sources are linked where they are used, and the example note is invented.

Are you allowed to record?

Policies vary by school and by instructor, so check yours before you press record. Four examples show the range:

  • The University of Minnesota’s Disability Resource Center audio recording FAQ says a blanket ban on recording does not override an accommodation, and that recordings made as an accommodation are for personal use only. It also says lecture recordings may not be shared with other people without the lecturer’s consent.
  • Missouri State’s recording policy gives qualifying students the right to record lectures only for their personal study, and says recordings may not be shared without the lecturer’s consent.
  • The University of Calgary’s calendar section E.6 says that, with the instructor’s permission, students without a formal accommodation may audio record lectures, for individual private study only. Other uses need the instructor’s written approval.
  • The University of Montana’s audio recording page says approved students must inform faculty before they record, must not share recordings without the instructor’s permission, and must delete them at the instructor’s request at the end of the term.

In practice: ask the instructor, or your disability services office if you have an accommodation. Keep recordings for your own study, and do not share or post them. This is general information, not legal advice.

Getting usable audio in a lecture hall

  • Sit near the front, or near the speaker or PA system if the room has one.
  • Put the phone face-up on the desk, not in a bag or pocket, with nothing resting on it.
  • Make a 30-second test at the start of class and play it back. Fixing a problem takes a minute now; it cannot be fixed after the lecture.
  • Check the battery and free storage before you leave for class.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so a notification sound does not land in the recording.

Questions asked by students in far seats will usually be weak or missing in the recording, because the phone is closer to the lecturer. Jot those down by hand.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Choose a transcription model. Automatic prefers Apple Speech on a supported iOS 26 device and language. You can instead download Whisper, Parakeet v2 (English) or Parakeet v3 (25 languages). Downloading a model is your choice and the only network step. Whisper vs Parakeet helps you choose, and offline transcription on iPhone covers preparing it.
  2. Make a Route per course. In Settings → Routes, create a Route named for the course, such as “BIO 201”. Choose a destination folder (an Obsidian vault or a Files folder) and New Note, so each lecture becomes its own file. Optionally add static frontmatter, one key: value per line, such as course: bio-201. The properties generator builds valid frontmatter.
  3. For a long class, record with a recorder you trust, then import it. Record the lecture in an app such as Voice Memos, then use the import control in Vox.md and choose Add to Draft so you can read the text before it is delivered. See transcribe Voice Memos to Markdown and transcribe a video to text for importing files.
  4. Or record in the Vox.md composer. That works too. Before you rely on it, test a full-length recording on your own device, and keep the app in the foreground while it records.
  5. Optional: Identify Speakers for seminars with discussion. It is opt-in on each Route and adds anonymous “Speaker 1” and “Speaker 2” labels, best effort. The first use prepares a speaker model, so do that before class.
  6. Review in Library. Library shows each capture; open it and check the text.

On a Mac

  • In person, with a laptop. Record with one of the options in how to record audio on a Mac, then choose Import Audio… in Vox.md for Mac. The steps are in transcribe an audio file on Mac.
  • An online lecture (a Zoom or Teams class). Meeting capture records one app’s audio and your microphone as separate stems. Both are written in durable chunks, so an interruption still leaves a recording that can be transcribed (see the Mac docs). Setup is in transcribe Mac meetings into Obsidian. Check your school’s rules on recording online classes too.
  • A caption file. If your learning platform or Zoom gave you an .srt or .vtt file, the SRT and VTT converter turns it into text or Markdown in your browser.

From transcript to study note

  1. Listen back and fix terms, names, formulas and numbers. Technical vocabulary is where transcripts most often need correcting.
  2. Add a heading per topic so you can scan the note before an exam.
  3. Pull out readings and deadlines into a checklist with the Markdown checklist maker.
  4. Put definitions in a table with the Markdown table maker.
  5. Link the note from your daily note; the daily note template is one way to set that up.

A finished note might look like this (invented sample):

---
course: bio-201
date: 2026-11-19
lecture: 7
---

## Key points
- Enzymes lower the activation energy of a reaction.
- Reading for next week: chapter 8.

## Terms
| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Substrate | The molecule an enzyme acts on |

## To do
- [ ] Read chapter 8
- [ ] Problem set due Friday

Troubleshooting

  • Quiet or far-away audio. Move closer next time, keep the phone on the desk face-up, and run the 30-second test. Weak audio gives a weaker transcript.
  • The wrong language. Parakeet v2 is English only. Use Parakeet v3 (25 languages) or Whisper for other languages; see Models.
  • The note didn’t arrive. Check the Route’s destination folder, then look in Library for the capture.
  • A garbled technical term. Fix it in your review pass, and use find and replace if it repeats.
  • No active plan. Recording, transcription and Capture need Vox.md Unlimited. The App Store price is authoritative.

FAQ

Can I record a lecture without permission?

It depends on your school and instructor. Ask first; see Are you allowed to record?

Is the audio uploaded?

Not with Vox.md’s on-device backends: Apple Speech, Whisper and Parakeet. Downloading a model is the only network step. If your destination folder syncs, such as iCloud Drive, the sync provider holds the file under its own terms. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once a model is prepared. See offline transcription on iPhone.

Can it tell the professor from students?

It can add anonymous, best-effort labels (“Speaker 1”, “Speaker 2”) if you turn on Identify Speakers. It does not recognise anyone by name. The method is the same as in transcribe an interview.

Does it summarise lectures?

This guide covers keeping a transcript as a note you review yourself, and makes no claims about summaries.
