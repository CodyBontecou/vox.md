The short answer

Apple’s Voice Memos and Notes apps can transcribe audio on a Mac for free, and for many people that is enough. To turn an existing audio or video file into a Markdown note, open Vox.md for Mac, choose Import Audio… from the menu bar (or import the file in the Capture workspace), let a local Whisper or Parakeet model transcribe it, review the text, then send it to your Route’s note.

Apple’s details below come from Apple Support’s pages View a transcription in Voice Memos on Mac and Record and transcribe audio in Notes on Mac, read on November 22, 2026. Apple’s pages can change, so check them for your Mac. The Vox.md steps follow the Mac documentation.

Free options already on your Mac

Voice Memos

Apple’s Voice Memos page says you can click the Transcription button while recording to see the text live, or select a finished recording and click the same button. You can search the recording list for text in a title or in the transcription, and copy transcript text by selecting it, Control-clicking and choosing Copy. Apple says that if you open a recording made in macOS 14 or earlier, or on a device that doesn’t support transcription, Voice Memos transcribes it automatically, and that audio transcription isn’t available in all countries or regions.

Notes

Apple’s Notes page describes recording audio inside a note: click the Attachment button, choose Record Audio, record, and click the Transcription button to see the transcript. It says audio transcription is available on Mac computers with M1 or later, for a device language that Apple lists on that page. If the Transcription button doesn’t appear, Apple says your Mac doesn’t meet the minimum requirements or doesn’t detect a supported language.

The limits

The text stays inside those apps until you copy it out. In Notes, Apple says deleting the audio recording also deletes the transcript. Which file types either app can bring in is not something we can source from Apple’s pages, so check them for your recording; this guide makes no claim about it.

As a last resort, some people play the audio and dictate it into a text editor. That is a workaround, not a file transcription; see dictation on Mac for dictation itself.

Prepare Vox.md for Mac

  1. Install. Vox.md for Mac needs macOS 14 or later. Get it from the App Store link below.
  2. Download a model. Open Settings → Models and download one: Whisper from Tiny (75 MB) up to Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB), Parakeet v2 (about 800 MB, English), or Parakeet v3 (about 800 MB, 25 languages). Set the transcription language on the same screen. Whisper uses Metal acceleration on supported Macs. The model download is the only network step. See Whisper vs Parakeet for choosing. whisper.cpp runs Whisper models and FluidAudio runs Parakeet.
  3. Pick or create a Route. A Route holds the destination folder or vault and the target note: a new note, a rolling daily note, or an existing note under a heading. See Routes in the docs.

Import and transcribe the file

  1. Import. From the Vox.md menu bar choose Import Audio…, or import or drag the audio into the Capture workspace. The docs say audio and movie files are accepted, and they go through the same local transcription as a recording.
  2. Choose Add to Draft or Send Immediately. With Send Immediately the transcript is delivered when it finishes. With Add to Draft you can review it first. Check names, numbers and dates against the audio.
  3. Optional: Identify Speakers. Turn on Identify Speakers on the Route for anonymous, best-effort speaker labels. It prepares a separate model the first time. If it can’t run, the plain transcript is kept and the reason shows in Library.
  4. Optional: processing mode. A Route can use Keep Original, Clean Prose, Meeting Notes, Todo Checklist or Custom Instruction. Apple Intelligence modes need compatible macOS 26 hardware. Without it, Todo Checklist and Meeting Notes use built-in formatters, and Clean Prose and Custom Instruction keep the original text.
  5. Send. Press Command-Return to send the capture to the Route’s note.

Check the Markdown note

Choose Reveal Last Export from the menu bar, or find the entry in Library and reveal it in Finder, then open the file in Obsidian. If you only need the text, choose Copy Last Transcript. For a note structure to fill from a recording, see the meeting notes template generator.

What leaves your Mac

Transcription runs on the Mac, and Vox.md does not send capture content to Vox.md servers. Model downloads contact Hugging Face or FluidAudio infrastructure. If the destination folder syncs through iCloud or another service, that service handles the file under its own terms. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

  • No transcript, or the wrong language. Check that a model is downloaded and selected, and the language is set, in Settings → Models.
  • No note appears. Check the Route’s destination folder and that Vox.md has access to it.
  • A long file takes a while. Larger models take more time on a long recording. Try a smaller model first, then compare.
  • Free allowance used up. It covers 15 minutes of transcription and 10 successful deliveries, then a one-time lifetime unlock. The App Store price is authoritative; see free use and lifetime unlock.
