Initial internal testing build of Vox.md for Android.

- Quick capture of typed Markdown, links, photos, files, scans, sketches, and voice
- Capture Presets with pinned quick-access rail and symbol, emoji, or initial icons
- On-device transcription with the bundled Whisper Small model
- Wear OS companion recording
- Local-first delivery to the Markdown folder or Obsidian vault you choose
