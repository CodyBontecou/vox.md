Vox.md 3.0 is a full redesign. The new Signal look and app icon put capture first. Live Notes autosave into a file in your vault, with a Files tree and an Activity timeline. Record in place with the compact capture dock, and switch routes from the pills in the top bar. Vox.md now runs on Wear OS: tap a preset on your watch and speak. Manage your plan from the new Subscription screen in Settings. Plus translation, accessibility and reliability fixes.
