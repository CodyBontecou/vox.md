Contact Vox.md

Contact the developer

For Vox.md support, bug reports, accessibility feedback, purchase questions, or privacy inquiries, email codybontecou@gmail.com. Vox.md is built by Cody Bontecou at Isolated Tech. This address is the direct public support contact for the app.

Include enough information to reproduce the problem

Tell us your Vox.md version, device model, operating system version, and the steps that led to the problem. Describe the expected result and what happened instead. For transcription issues, include the selected speech backend and language. For file delivery issues, describe the type of destination and Capture Preset settings using a small sample that is safe to share.

Keep private notes, recordings, transcripts, attachments, exact locations, folder paths, and account information out of your initial message. The app processes capture content locally; support does not have access to your vault or app history. Share only a minimal example you are comfortable sending.

Documentation, purchases, and community

The feature guide includes setup instructions and troubleshooting. Apple manages App Store billing and purchase restoration. Current purchase options and localized prices appear in the App Store listing.

For source code and public development discussion, visit Vox.md on GitHub. See About Vox.md for the product and developer background, and the privacy policy for data handling details.
