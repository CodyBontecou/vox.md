About Vox.md

Capture into the notes you already own

Vox.md is a local capture app for people who use Obsidian vaults or Markdown folders. It is built by Cody Bontecou at Isolated Tech. Capture a thought, link, photo, file, scan, sketch, or voice note and send it to a precise destination without first searching through your notes.

Vox.md works across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, with features and minimum system versions documented in the complete feature guide. Capture Presets define the folder, note, template, and insertion position. The app writes standard Markdown and attachments, so your chosen files remain useful in other editors.

Built for private, local workflows

Voice transcription runs on the Apple device using a supported Apple Speech backend or an optional downloaded Whisper or Parakeet model. No Vox.md account or Obsidian plugin is required. Capture content is written to the folder you select; your own storage provider may sync that folder. Read the privacy policy for the details of models, permissions, optional location, and external services.

Try Vox.md and get help

The free download includes 15 minutes of transcription and 10 successful Capture deliveries. Unlimited use is available as a lifetime purchase; current localized prices are shown by Apple in the App Store. Get Vox.md on the App Store, read the setup guide, or contact the developer.

Vox.md is open source. You can inspect the implementation and license in the GitHub repository. This public website provides product information and documentation; capture actions run locally in the installed app through its interface, Shortcuts, and documented URL scheme.
