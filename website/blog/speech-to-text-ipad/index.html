How to use speech to text on iPad, with a private local option

Turn on dictation, find the mic key when a keyboard is attached, fix it when it does not work, and decide whether you want a local model or a Markdown note instead.

By
Cody Bontecou
Updated
November 24, 2026
Requires
Vox.md: iOS/iPadOS 17.6 or later. Automatic Apple Speech needs a supported iOS 26 device and language. Apple Dictation availability depends on your device, iPadOS version and language.
The short answer

Go to Settings > General > Keyboard, turn on Enable Dictation, then tap the Dictate button on the onscreen keyboard. With a Magic Keyboard or Smart Keyboard attached, Apple says to press the Switch Keyboard key and D, or tap the Dictation button in the Shortcuts bar at the bottom of the screen. If you want a local Whisper or Parakeet model, or speech saved to a Markdown note, use the Vox.md keyboard or a Vox.md Route.

Apple’s steps below come from the iPad User Guide on Apple Support: Dictate text on iPad, Dictate text while using an Apple external keyboard with iPad, Commands for dictating text and iPad Magic Keyboard and Smart Keyboard. I read them on November 24, 2026, when the guide listed iPadOS 27 as its newest version. Menu names can differ between iPadOS versions, so check the pages for yours. The Vox.md steps follow the keyboard and models documentation. The general dictation steps are the same as on the phone, so this guide keeps them short and spends its words on what is different on iPad; the full phone walkthrough is speech to text on iPhone. On a Mac? See dictation on Mac.

How to turn on speech to text on iPad

  1. Open Settings, tap General, then tap Keyboard.
  2. Turn on Enable Dictation. If a prompt appears, tap Enable Dictation again.
  3. In a text field, tap to place the insertion point.
  4. Tap the Dictate button on the onscreen keyboard. When the Dictate button appears near the insertion point, start speaking.
  5. Stop: tap the Dictate button again, say “Stop dictation,” or stay silent for 30 seconds, after which Apple says Dictation stops automatically.

Apple says the keyboard (or tools palette) can stay open while you dictate, so you can mix typing and speaking. To turn Dictation off, go back to Settings > General > Keyboard and turn off Enable Dictation. Apple also says cellular data charges may apply.

Dictate with a Magic Keyboard, Smart Keyboard or other keyboard

Apple has a separate page for this, Dictate text while using an Apple external keyboard with iPad. As it reads on November 24, 2026:

  1. Turn on dictation first: Settings > General > Keyboard > Enable Dictation.
  2. Tap the Dictation button in the Shortcuts bar at the bottom of the screen, then speak. Apple says iPad inserts punctuation automatically, and you can add an emoji by saying its name, such as “happy emoji.”
  3. When you finish, tap the Keyboard button at the bottom of the screen.

The shortcut: Apple says that on the Magic Keyboard for iPad or the Smart Keyboard, you can press the Switch Keyboard key and D to switch to dictation quickly. Apple lists which iPad models each keyboard works with on iPad Magic Keyboard and Smart Keyboard.

Apple’s page describes the Apple external keyboards only. If you use another brand and the shortcut or the Shortcuts bar button does not appear, use the onscreen keyboard’s Dictate button instead, or try Vox.md Quick Capture, which has its own microphone button (see below). Vox.md does not document its keyboard for use while a hardware keyboard is attached.

Punctuation and formatting

A few examples from Apple’s Commands for dictating text: say the name of a mark such as “comma” or “question mark”; say “new line” or “new paragraph” for breaks; say “cap” to capitalize the next word; say “heart emoji” for an emoji; and say “undo” or “redo.” Apple lists the full set, including editing commands that vary by language and device, on that page. The iPhone commands are covered in the iPhone guide.

Does iPad dictation work offline?

Apple says Dictation is processed on your iPad in many languages, and no internet connection is required. When you dictate in a search field, Apple says your dictated text may be sent to the search engine to process the search. Apple also says Dictation may not be available in all languages or in all countries or regions, and features may vary. Check Apple’s iOS and iPadOS Feature Availability page for your language.

Apple’s page adds that with Apple Intelligence in iPadOS 27, dictation uses an on-device model to improve spelling, punctuation and capitalization, available in English on iPad models with M4 or later and at least 12GB of unified memory. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions, and usage limits may apply.

Use a local model in any iPad app with the Vox.md keyboard

The getting started docs list the keyboard for both iPhone and iPad.

  1. Prepare transcription. In Vox.md, keep Automatic on a supported device, or download and select Whisper or Parakeet in Settings > Models.
  2. Add the keyboard. In iPadOS Settings, go to General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard and choose Vox.md.
  3. Turn on Allow Full Access. It is required: the current keyboard workflow needs it to use shared App Group state and coordinate recording with the main app. iOS labels the permission broadly; Vox.md uses it for its on-device app and keyboard workflow, shared state and optional haptics, and does not upload keystrokes, dictated text, transcripts or microphone audio to Vox.md servers. See the keyboard setup docs.
  4. Switch and dictate. In a text field, use the globe key to select Vox.md, then tap the microphone.

With Automatic, finalized text is inserted as you speak. With Whisper or Parakeet, the text is inserted after you stop. The docs describe the keyboard for apps that accept a standard iOS keyboard; they do not cover using it with a hardware keyboard attached or in Stage Manager, Split View or Slide Over, so use Apple Dictation or Vox.md Quick Capture in those situations. To pick a model, read Whisper vs Parakeet.

Speech to text straight into Obsidian or a Markdown file

Choose this when the words belong in a note rather than another app. Open Quick Capture, which the docs call the main iOS and iPadOS workspace, and use inline voice. Add to Draft appends the finished transcript to your draft; Send Immediately runs the selected Route and its destination. A Route can target a daily or rolling note, a heading and a processing mode. Start with voice notes to Obsidian (the steps are the same on iPad), then see appending to a daily note. For existing audio, see offline transcription to Markdown. Quick Capture can also hold a PencilKit sketch; for paper notes, see handwritten notes to Obsidian. Eligible accounts get a 7-day free trial.

What stays on the iPad

Vox.md does not send capture content to Vox.md servers. Transcription runs on your iPad. The only network use is the model download you start yourself, which uses Hugging Face or FluidAudio infrastructure. If a note lands in a folder that syncs through iCloud or another service, that service handles the file under its own terms. See the privacy policy.

iPad dictation not working

Apple Dictation checks

  • No Dictate button. Go to Settings > General > Keyboard and turn on Enable Dictation, as Apple’s page says.
  • A keyboard is attached and the onscreen keyboard is gone. Use the Dictation button in the Shortcuts bar at the bottom of the screen, or the Switch Keyboard key and D on a Magic Keyboard or Smart Keyboard, per Apple’s external-keyboard page. Tap the Keyboard button when you finish.
  • Language. Dictation may not be available in all languages or regions. See Apple’s feature availability page.
  • It stopped. Apple says Dictation stops automatically after 30 seconds of silence. Start it again.

Vox.md checks

  • The keyboard says Enable Full Access. Open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Vox.md and turn on Allow Full Access.
  • The keyboard says Open Vox.md. If listening is not active, the keyboard opens Vox.md once to start listening.
  • No transcript. Make sure a model is fully downloaded and selected in Settings > Models.

More in the Vox.md troubleshooting docs.

Questions

Is iPad dictation free?

Apple’s page doesn’t mention a charge, though it says cellular data charges may apply. Vox.md needs Vox.md Unlimited: a weekly or yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for eligible accounts, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does it need the internet?

Apple says Dictation is processed on the iPad in many languages with no internet connection required, except that text dictated into a search field may be sent to the search engine. Once a model is downloaded, Vox.md transcribes on the iPad.

Can I dictate with the Magic Keyboard?

Yes. Apple says to tap the Dictation button in the Shortcuts bar, or press the Switch Keyboard key and D on a Magic Keyboard or Smart Keyboard. See the section above.

Can I pick Whisper or Parakeet on iPad?

Yes, in Vox.md Settings > Models, for the keyboard and for Routes. See Whisper vs Parakeet.

Can speech go straight into Obsidian?

Yes. Use inline voice in Quick Capture with a Route that delivers to a note in your vault, as in the voice notes guide.
