Description of service

Vox.md is a quick capture application for iOS and macOS with keyboard, share, widget, and Shortcuts integrations. It writes text, links, images, files, scans, sketches, and voice captures into user-selected Markdown folders, including Obsidian vaults. Optional voice-to-text uses local speech recognition models on your device.

These models and system services are provided "as is," and we make no guarantees regarding the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of transcription output.

System asset preparation and optional local model downloads may require an internet connection and consume device storage. Transcription can run locally after the selected backend is ready. You are responsible for managing your device storage and data usage related to optional downloads.

Accuracy disclaimer

Voice-to-text transcription is performed by machine learning models and is inherently imperfect. Vox.md does not guarantee the accuracy of any transcription. Transcription quality may vary based on:

  • The Whisper model size selected (Tiny through Large v3 Turbo)
  • Audio quality, background noise, and microphone hardware
  • Speaker accent, cadence, and language
  • Device processing capabilities

Do not rely on Vox.md for critical, legal, medical, or safety-related transcription without independently verifying the output.
