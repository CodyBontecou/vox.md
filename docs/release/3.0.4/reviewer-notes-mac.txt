Vox.md 3.0.4 (macOS). No account or sign-in: there is no login, and no demo account is needed. Please test purchases with your App Review sandbox Apple Account.

PAID ACCESS AND TRIAL
New captures and transcription require paid access. The paywall offers Individual or Family, weekly or yearly (group Vox.md Unlimited), plus lifetime purchases; iPhone and Mac share them. Eligible sandbox accounts get a 7-day free trial on the subscriptions; Apple decides eligibility. Lifetime has no trial. Restore Purchases restores earlier lifetime purchases and paid-app ownership. Manage Subscription, Terms and Privacy links are available.
Without paying, View and Export Your Library on the paywall opens existing notes to read, search, export and delete. It cannot make new recordings or captures.

HOW TO TEST
1. Start the free trial (or restore) in the sandbox.
2. Create or choose a Route and pick a writable folder (e.g. a folder in Documents).
3. Type a short note and click Send; a Markdown file is written to that folder.
4. For voice, allow microphone access, record, stop and send. Transcription runs on the Mac with Apple Speech or optional downloaded Whisper/Parakeet models.
5. Optional (new in 3.0.4): set a Route to Remote-only with a URL; the capture is sent only to that URL and no file is written.

RATING PROMPT
After several captures sent from the main window on two days, the app may ask for a rating with Apple's standard requestReview API, at most once per version. It never blocks a feature.

PRIVACY
Audio, transcripts and note contents stay on the Mac and are written only to folders the user chooses. The app has no custom usage analytics or ad attribution. Optional URL delivery sends the capture only to an endpoint the user enters.
