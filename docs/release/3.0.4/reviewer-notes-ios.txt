Vox.md 3.0.4 (iOS). No account or sign-in: there is no login, and no demo account is needed. Please test purchases with your App Review sandbox Apple Account.

PAID ACCESS AND TRIAL
New captures and transcription require paid access. On first use the paywall offers Individual or Family, weekly or yearly (group Vox.md Unlimited), plus lifetime purchases. Eligible sandbox accounts get a 7-day free trial on the subscriptions; Apple decides eligibility. Lifetime has no trial. Restore Purchases restores earlier lifetime purchases and paid-app ownership. Manage Subscriptions, Terms and Privacy links are on the paywall.
Without paying, the Library button (clock icon) at the top of the paywall opens existing notes to read, search, copy, export and delete. It cannot make new recordings or captures.

HOW TO TEST
1. Start the free trial (or restore) in the sandbox.
2. Create or choose a Capture Preset and pick a writable folder (e.g. On My iPhone).
3. Type a short note and tap Send; a Markdown file is written to that folder.
4. For voice, allow microphone access, record, stop and send. Transcription runs on the device with Apple Speech or optional downloaded Whisper/Parakeet models.
5. Optional: Settings › Recording Quality › High and Stereo records stereo with the built-in microphones (new in 3.0.4).

KEYBOARD
Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Vox.md, then turn on Allow Full Access. Full Access is required solely for microphone access in the keyboard extension. No keystrokes or dictated text are collected or transmitted.

RATING PROMPT
After several delivered captures on two days, the app may ask for a rating with Apple's standard requestReview API, at most once per version. It never blocks a feature.

PRIVACY
Audio, transcripts and note contents stay on the device and are written only to folders the user chooses. The app has no custom usage analytics or ad attribution. Optional URL delivery (including Remote-only Routes) sends the capture only to an endpoint the user enters.
